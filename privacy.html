Watch Accuracy privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · WorldLamp Apps · worldlampapps@gmail.com

What the app collects: nothing

Watch Accuracy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your data.

What stays on your device or in your iCloud

  • The watches you add (names, calibres, notes, photos), your sync readings and your timegrapher results are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and which we cannot access.
  • Your 14-day trial start date and your Pro purchase status are stored on your device; purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store.

Microphone

The timegrapher listens to the ticking of your watch while the screen is open. Audio is analysed on the device in real time and is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Network

The app contacts public time servers (time.apple.com, time.google.com, pool.ntp.org) to obtain atomic time. The request contains no personal data. iCloud sync communicates only with Apple’s iCloud.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the policy changes. Questions: worldlampapps@gmail.com.
