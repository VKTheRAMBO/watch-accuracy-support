Watch Accuracy support

Watch Accuracy for iPhone and iPad measures how your mechanical watch really runs: seconds per day against atomic time, plus an acoustic timegrapher (rate, beat error, amplitude) and a guided six-position test. It is published by WorldLamp Apps.

Quick answers

How do I sync?

Open a watch, tap Sync, watch the seconds hand and tap the instant it reaches the chosen mark. Two or three taps give a better reading. Sync again after a day or more to get the daily rate.

The timegrapher finds no ticks

Use a quiet room, press the case back firmly against the microphone at the bottom edge of the phone, and wait 10 seconds. A piezo contact microphone through the headphone adapter gives a far cleaner trace. No watch handy? Tap “Try a demo signal”.

Which beat rate?

Auto detects 18,000 to 36,000 vph. If the app says the ticks disagree with the watch’s preset, accept the detected value.

What do the grades mean?

Excellent under 5 s/day, Good under 10, Acceptable under 20. COSC chronometers must stay within −4/+6 s/day. A sudden jump to +60 or more usually means the watch is magnetised.

iCloud sync

Turn it on in Settings → iCloud on each device signed in to the same Apple ID. The status line explains if sync is not running.
